Family-owned. Colour first.

Salon Milly Rose is a boutique hair studio run by a family, on Hall Drive in Menai. We cut and style, but blondes and colour correction are what we’re known for.

Our colour is made to last, look good and be worth what you pay. Come and see the space: the arches, the cactus garden and the light.

We colour with Redken, and we’re open till 9pm on Wednesdays and Thursdays.
